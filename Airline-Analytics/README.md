Project Background:
SkyHigh Airlines, a mid-sized international airline, wants to improve operational efficiency and enhance customer satisfaction. The management team has asked for a Power BI dashboard to help them analyze flights, delays, passenger satisfaction, and revenue trends.

Objectives:
1.	Analyze overall airline performance.
2.	Track flight punctuality and identify delay trends.
3.	Evaluate passenger satisfaction scores.
4.	Understand top-performing routes and airports.
5.	Provide KPIs to help decision-makers plan for improvements.

Required Visualizations & KPIs
 
 Dashboard 1: Executive Summary

•	Total Flights, Total Passengers, Total Revenue 
•	Average Delay 
•	Flights by Status
•	Monthly Revenue Trend 
•	Map of routes with number of flights 

Dashboard 2: Flight Operations

•	Average Delay by Route 
•	Delay Trend by Month 
•	Aircraft Type Usage 
•	Top 5 Delayed Airports 

Dashboard 3: Customer Insights

•	Average Passenger Satisfaction Score 
•	Feedback Score Distribution 
•	Ticket Class Breakdown (Age Group or Gender)
•	Frequent Flyers vs Satisfaction Score (Scatter Plot)
